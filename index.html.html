About Madhavan Group

Powering Green Energy Capital and Turnkey Solar Engineering

**Madhavan Group** (Madhavan Green Energy / MGE) operates at the strategic intersection of renewable energy development, project acquisitions, and IPP asset expansion. Headquartered in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, we originate, develop, and acquire high-yield solar assets to power India’s clean grid transition.

Under the leadership of Founder **Anshu Pachauri**, MGE has cultivated robust competencies in government solar tender executions (including PM-KUSUM and Maharashtra's flagship MSKVY 2.0 scheme), regulatory approvals, land banking, grid interconnectivity, and long-term financial structuring.
