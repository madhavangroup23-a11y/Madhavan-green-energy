Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information you may provide when visiting the Madhavan Green Energy website, how it may be used, and how you can contact us about your information.

Effective date: 22 November 2026
Important information: This page describes the website's visible enquiry form and the intended handling of information submitted to us. The actual collection, transmission, storage and deletion of enquiry data depend on the form service, hosting configuration and other tools connected to the live website.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Madhavan Green Energy (MGE) website operated under the Madhavan Group name. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Madhavan Green Energy (MGE). It applies to information handled through this website and communications initiated through it.

2. Information you may provide

Our Investor & Partner Inquiry form displays fields for the following information:

  • Full name
  • Company or organisation
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Inquiry type, such as a joint venture or other business enquiry
  • Message, requirements or other details you choose to provide

Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers or other sensitive personal information in the message field.

When you contact us by email or phone, we may also receive the contact details and information that you choose to share in that communication.

3. How we use information

Where information is submitted to and received by us, we may use it to:

  • Respond to investor, partner, joint-venture and business enquiries;
  • Review project proposals, asset acquisition opportunities and consultation requests;
  • Contact you to clarify or follow up on your enquiry;
  • Maintain business communication records and manage our relationship with you;
  • Protect the website, prevent misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not state that information is collected or stored merely because a form is displayed. Information is received by us only if the live form and its connected services successfully transmit it to us.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, such as with service providers that host the website, operate an enquiry form, provide business communications or support website security. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or a lawful request from an authority.

Any service provider that handles information should do so only for its authorised service purpose and subject to appropriate safeguards and contractual arrangements, where applicable.

5. Cookies and technical information

The website's hosting provider or technical services may process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, access time and server logs, for delivery, security, troubleshooting and reliability. Whether cookies, analytics or similar technologies are used depends on the services enabled on the live website.

If analytics, advertising, embedded content or additional tracking tools are added, this policy should be updated to identify those tools and explain the choices available to visitors. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

6. Data security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved. No website, internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please contact us promptly if you believe information you sent through this website has been exposed or misused.

7. Data retention

We intend to retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and manage the enquiry, maintain relevant business records, resolve disputes and comply with legal requirements. Actual retention periods depend on the systems receiving and storing the information and the applicable legal obligations.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided, request correction of inaccurate details, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, or raise a concern about how your information is handled. We may need to verify your identity and the details of your request before acting. Some records may need to be retained where required or permitted by law.

We will handle requests in accordance with applicable data protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules to the extent their relevant provisions apply.

9. Third-party websites and services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party hosting and other services. Those third parties operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review their policies before providing information to them. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites.

10. Children's privacy

This website is intended for business, investor and professional enquiries and is not directed at children. Please do not submit a child's personal information through this website. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, data practices or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Please review this page periodically.
